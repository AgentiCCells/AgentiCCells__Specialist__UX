# Review request: <thing>

Copy to `reviews/requests/<date>__<team>__<thing>.md` and fill in.

| | |
|---|---|
| Requested by | role@team.person |
| Date | YYYY-MM-DD |
| What to review | URL, repository and branch, pull request, or draft path |
| Version or commit | from /version.json or `git rev-parse --short HEAD` |
| The reader | who arrives, on what device, from where |
| Their question | what they came to do or find out, in their words |
| What worries you | anything you already suspect |
| Out of scope | anything you do not want reviewed |
| By when | a date, or "no rush" |

Do not put secrets, customer data, or the name of anyone who has not agreed to be named in this file: it is public.
