# UX guidance

Version 1.0.0, 16 November 2026. Kept by specialist.ux@AgentiCCells.dinis; the skill's owner is Dinis Cruz, who decides what changes.

The guidance is a compression of what readers and users tripped on across our sites and apps from September to November 2026: promptugal.news, the storm site, the Web Summit team site, newsroom.sgit.ai, riskmandate.ai, the PrompTugal iOS apps and the AgentiCCells sites. Every principle carries its source.

| Page | What it is | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| [Principles](principles.md) | Thirty principles, each with the trip behind it, its source and what to do | writing or designing anything a reader sees |
| [Checklist](checklist.md) | The checks to run before a release, most of them measurable | before every release |
| [Methods](methods.md) | How to run a cold-reader review (the sherpa method) and synthetic readers | setting up review in your team |
| [Examples](../examples/README.md) | The best pages and patterns we have, with links | you want to see one done well |

| [The pack](../pack/README.md) | What a new team copies on day one | starting a team |

## The ten that matter most

If you read nothing else:

1. The first screen says what this is, who made it, and what to do next ([1](principles.md#1-the-first-screen-says-what-this-is-who-made-it-and-what-to-do-next)).
2. Write in the reader's words, not the team's ([5](principles.md#5-write-in-the-reader-s-words-not-the-team-s)).
3. A label never says more than its source ([8](principles.md#8-a-label-never-says-more-than-its-source)).
4. Say what is not here ([11](principles.md#11-say-what-is-not-here)).
5. Freshness is part of the fact ([14](principles.md#14-freshness-is-part-of-the-fact)).
6. Never show an invitation without a working route ([15](principles.md#15-never-show-an-invitation-control-or-promise-without-a-working-route)).
7. A link says where it goes ([17](principles.md#17-a-link-says-where-it-goes)).
8. Measure at 390 px ([21](principles.md#21-measure-at-390-px-nothing-panning-covered-or-cut)).
9. Length is a cost the reader pays ([22](principles.md#22-length-is-a-cost-the-reader-pays)).
10. After any fix, re-measure the whole first screen at the narrowest width ([28](principles.md#28-after-any-fix-re-measure-the-whole-first-screen-at-the-narrowest-width)).
