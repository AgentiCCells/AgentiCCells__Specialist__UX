# Mistakes we made more than once

A mistake made once is a finding. These were made in several teams, which means the cause is in how we work, not in one page. Each has its instances, its process cause, and what stops it. Sources are in the [principles](../guidance/principles.md).

### Agents write for agents

**Seen in.** Agent addresses as bylines; "storyline S2" and "403" in "Who made this" (20 occurrences); "116 releases"; internal node ids as graph labels; "1,688 nodes, drawn natively"; "on Dinis's list" on a public storm page; "About" opening an internal brief; a box asking visitors for a key.

**Cause.** Pages are generated by agents from the team's own data, and agents live in the team's words. One page frame often serves both team and public. The cold read happens after release, if at all.

**Stops it.** A wording pass from a cold reader before release ([checklist](../guidance/checklist.md#words)); internal pages one level down and labelled.

### A fix causes the next trip

**Seen in.** The credit fix cut the site's name; jumping to "now" hid the day row; naming the agent brought back the email look; a fifth button made the phone header worse; a cut graph became an unmarked sideways scroll; each storm guide fix pushed another answer down.

**Cause.** Fixes were verified against the one finding, often on a desktop. As synthetic@PrompTugal.dinis wrote: "an owner's 'done' needs measuring on the phone, not only the desktop".

**Stops it.** Re-measure the whole first screen at 390 px after every fix ([principle 28](../guidance/principles.md#28-after-any-fix-re-measure-the-whole-first-screen-at-the-narrowest-width)); the reviewer, not the fixer, marks it done.

### A true page goes stale the same day

**Seen in.** agenticcells.site says no sites live on its subdomains while ten answer; the Web Summit home page says "The summit is on" a week after; a storm page "Last checked" nine hours earlier on a red-warning morning; "We have not asked this company yet", false the day the email lands; a findings page listing as urgent two findings its own ledger marks fixed; REALITY files that say existing things are missing.

**Cause.** Text written by hand once, when it was true, and never regenerated. REALITY files appended rather than rewritten. Nothing tells a page's owner when the world it describes changes, and pages carry no expiry.

**Stops it.** Generate lists and counts from the record; date every "today" line; an archive banner after an event; rewrite REALITY sections in place ([principles 14 and 29](../guidance/principles.md#14-freshness-is-part-of-the-fact)).

### Invitations with no route

**Seen in.** "Tell Dinis Cruz" with no link (open for ten days); a newsletter with no way to ask to be added; GitHub as the only way to correct something; printed cards promising "Book a meeting"; "Play the briefing" that played nothing; a press visitor on the storm site with "nobody to call".

**Cause.** The route waited on a decision only one person could make, and the copy shipped before the route existed. Agents asked instead of proposing a default and removing the invitation meanwhile.

**Stops it.** No invitation without a working route ([principle 15](../guidance/principles.md#15-never-show-an-invitation-control-or-promise-without-a-working-route)). If it waits on a decision, take it off and propose the default.

### Pages grow until the answer is screens down

**Seen in.** A team dashboard 30,705 px tall; a blocking ask at row 53 of 93, in file order; a findings page of 64 phone screens; a trust page of 64,292 px; a storm front page of 11,000 px all day.

**Cause.** Agents append every run; lists render in full and in file order; nobody owns removal; each new request becomes a new view.

**Stops it.** Answers first, newest five then "Earlier (N)", caps on lists, page height measured every release, and a new view names the one it replaces ([principles 19 and 22](../guidance/principles.md#22-length-is-a-cost-the-reader-pays)).

### One page, several stories

**Seen in.** "No person read this" beside "Edited by" three people; "OUR PICK" beside "not an endorsement"; £4.97 and £4.49 on one screen; "Get in touch" and "Book a demo" for the same button; "9 agents" against an inventory of 16; a site description promising "who we met" while its brief says "invent no meetings".

**Cause.** Parts of a page come from different agents, templates and times: footer, byline, tagline, image, meta description. No single source feeds them.

**Stops it.** One source per fact; read the whole page, footer and meta included, as one text before release ([principle 13](../guidance/principles.md#13-one-page-tells-one-story)).

### Built and judged on a desktop

**Seen in.** Articles 591 px wide on a 390 px phone; sections behind an unmarked swipe; "now" buried 6,000 px down on a phone; a floating button over every headline; a price 1,025 px down on a phone; a time wrapping "11:05 A / M".

**Cause.** Designs drawn and reviewed at desktop width; the phone order is a separate rule nobody owns until a check names it.

**Stops it.** Every review starts on the phone ([methods](../guidance/methods.md)); acceptance checks at 390 px written before the build.

### Empty and changed states not designed

**Seen in.** "Nothing matches on this day" at the venue; a called-off day listed with "Add" buttons; an unknown time as "All day"; an offline page that looked like an error; an offline talk with its data on the phone that would not open.

**Cause.** Specs describe the full, happy state. Empty and changed states are found live, one surface at a time, and several builds render the same fact from different code.

**Stops it.** Every spec lists its empty, offline and changed states; a change is checked on every way in ([principles 24 and 25](../guidance/principles.md#24-design-the-empty-offline-and-changed-states)).

### Gates that pass a broken page

**Seen in.** riskmandate.ai: a script error on every page while 26 tests and six gates passed; ten newsroom articles showing "screenshot unavailable" while the gate was green; a validator that checks a link resolves but not what it says; screenshots of a debug build; a screenshot tool that overwrote its own images.

**Cause.** Gates check structure, not what a reader meets in a browser. Automated evidence trusted without a sanity check.

**Stops it.** A real browser in the release check, page errors recorded, and a person-or-agent look at the screenshots ([principle 27](../guidance/principles.md#27-check-in-a-real-browser-with-real-data-the-console-counts)).

### Findings with no one to decide them

**Seen in.** 32 of 40 fixes undecided five hours after a review; a priority ask undecided for four days; a newcomer page left as "a draft for Dinis" until the hold.

**Cause.** One approver for many agents, and findings that did not say who decides. What worked: each owner writing accept, decline (with why), later or done in their own file.

**Stops it.** Every finding names one owner and the default that happens if nobody decides.
