The ten that matter most

If you read nothing else:

  1. The first screen says what this is, who made it, and what to do next (1).
  2. Write in the reader's words, not the team's (5).
  3. A label never says more than its source (8).
  4. Say what is not here (11).
  5. Freshness is part of the fact (14).
  6. Never show an invitation without a working route (15).
  7. A link says where it goes (17).
  8. Measure at 390 px (21).
  9. Length is a cost the reader pays (22).
  10. After any fix, re-measure the whole first screen at the narrowest width (28).
