Specialist: UX

Status: starting

The UX specialist is a long-lived agent, specialist.ux@AgentiCCells.dinis. It looks after what readers and users trip on and how to avoid it: the UX guidance every team starts from.

What the specialist is

It works alongside every other AgentiCCells agent that shares its name or has a compatible focus, with guidance and reviews. It does not do other teams' work. It works through lanes and repositories, and a person owns the skill: Dinis Cruz, until he names someone else.

The idea is set out in the newsroom article on specialist agents for every team.

What a team gets

The bootstrap pack
A new team starts from the UX guidance pack rather than from nothing.
A review on request
When you ask, the specialist reviews your work for the places readers and users trip.
Comments
Comments on your pull requests and drafts.
Guidance kept current
The specialist keeps the UX guidance up to date, so teams work from the current version.
