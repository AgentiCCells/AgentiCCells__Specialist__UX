Specialist: UX

What readers and users trip on, and how to avoid it. A long-lived agent that gives every AgentiCCells team its UX guidance, a day-one pack, and reviews on request. It never does a team's work; it comments and proposes.

Status: first version, 16 November 2026

Start here

  • The day-one pack
    Starting a team? Copy a UX.md, a spec template and a measuring script. Ten minutes.
  • Thirty principles
    Each from a trip a reader really had on one of our sites, with its source.
  • The checklist
    What to check before every release, most of it measurable.
  • Ask for a review
    How to ask, what you get back, and how long it takes.

What is here

  • Guidance: principles, the checklist, and two review methods (the cold reader and synthetic readers).
  • Examples: the best pages and patterns we have, and why they work.
  • Mistakes: what went wrong in more than one team, and the process cause.
  • A worked review: agenticcells.site, seven findings, written as a proposal.
  • This agent's behaviour policy: deny by default; what it may read, write, review and comment on, and what it never does.

Every page has a Markdown twin beside it (the same address ending in .md), for agents.

Where the guidance comes from

From reading, on 16 November 2026, what our teams recorded about readers and users from September to November 2026: the synthetic readers and the sherpa reviews on promptugal.news, the storm site, the Web Summit team site, newsroom.sgit.ai, riskmandate.ai, the PrompTugal iOS apps, and the AgentiCCells sites. Most of those findings come from agents reading pages cold, which is inspection, not user research.

The idea is set out in the newsroom article on specialist agents for every team. The skill's owner is Dinis Cruz, until he names someone else.

What is not here yet

No lane for reviews yet: ask through a file in this repository or a message to this session (see Reviews). No commenting on other teams' pull requests yet: this agent's GitHub access covers only its own repository. Turnaround times are targets, not yet measured.
